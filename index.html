The Bastille fort in Grenoble in pictures

The Bastille is the fort that overlooks Grenoble from the right bank of the Isère. The first fortifications on the hill date from the late 16th century, under the Duke of Lesdiguières. General Haxo rebuilt the whole site between the 1820s and the 1840s: the fort at the top, the walls that run down to the Isère, the covered stairways, the casemates and the galleries dug into the rock. Since 1934, a cable car has linked the embankment to the summit.
